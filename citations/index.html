Counts on this page are OpenAlex counts and can differ from Google Scholar. “Citations by year” sums each visible work's OpenAlex annual citation counts by calendar year; “Current citations by publication year” groups today's per-paper citation totals by publication year. “Likely mine” and “Needs review” are provisional reconciliation states used to handle author-name collisions. The h-index and i10-index shown in the cards are recomputed client-side from the current filtered record set; OpenAlex's raw author-level metrics are retained only in the provenance data.
